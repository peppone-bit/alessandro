Status Epilepticus History

8 documented SE episodes — the first in Foggia, Italy in July 2018 (age 6 months), 6 clustered October 2020 through February 2021, and one breakthrough in March 2022 that led to Fintepla introduction. Seizure-free from March 2022 on current regimen. Multiple episodes required intubation and ICU admission. Buccolam at home frequently provides only transient control — recurrence after initial response is a consistent pattern. Early IV escalation is appropriate.
